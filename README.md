##  Banking Analytics: Customer Segmentation & Risk Insights

>  Transforming raw banking data into actionable insights for smarter financial decisions

---

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes banking data using `Python`, `MySQL`, and `Power BI` to uncover insights into:

- Customer behavior  
- Loan distribution  
- Deposit trends  
- Financial performance  

 **Goal:** Enable data-driven decision-making and risk optimization.

---

##  Objectives

-  Analyze total `loans` and `deposits`  
-  Identify **high-value customer segments**  
-  Evaluate loan distribution across income groups  
-  Understand demographic impact  
-  Provide insights for **risk management**

---

##  Business Problem

>    Banks handle massive data but struggle to identify:

-  Revenue-driving customers  
-  Loan risk distribution  
-  Deposit vs Loan imbalance (**liquidity risk**)  
-  Demographic influence on financial behavior

##  Key Insights

###  Overall Performance
-  **Total Loans:** `$4.4B`  
-  **Total Deposits:** `$3.77B`  

>  **Loans exceed deposits → Liquidity Risk**

---

###  Loan Analysis
-  Private Banking dominates  
   Medium-income group → `~53% of loans`  

>  **High concentration risk**

---

###   Deposit Analysis
-  Medium-income group → `~54% of deposits`  

>  **Over-dependence on one segment**

---

###  Customer Insights
-  European customers dominate  
-  Uneven occupation distribution  

>  **Lack of diversification**

---

##  Key Problems Identified

-  Loan > Deposit imbalance (**Liquidity Risk**)  
-  Over-reliance on medium-income customers  
-  Private banking concentration  
-  Limited demographic diversity  
-  Uneven loan distribution (**Credit Risk**)  

---

##  Recommended Solutions

-  Improve **deposit mobilization strategies**  
-  Diversify loan portfolio  
-  Strengthen risk management policies  
-  Target low & high-income segments  
-  Expand into underrepresented markets
